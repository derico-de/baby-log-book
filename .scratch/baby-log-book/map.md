# Map: Baby Log Book

Label: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A **locked spec plus ADRs** for the Baby Log Book PWA — domain model, feature scope and phasing, stack and storage decisions, offline/sync semantics, auth model, and UI direction — complete enough to slice into implementation tickets and build mostly AFK.

The map ends when the spec exists and nothing architectural is still undecided. Building v1 is a separate effort.

**Reached.** The spec is at [`spec.md`](spec.md), the decisions are in [`docs/adr/`](../../docs/adr/) and the vocabulary is in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md). No tickets remain open and the frontier is empty. What is left in **Not yet specified** is v2 and v3 work that sits past this destination — it does not graduate here, and picking any of it up means drawing a new map.

## Notes

**Domain**: a self-hosted household log for babies — feeds, sleep, nappies, solids, growth measurements — used by parents and grandparents, most often one-handed on a phone at 3am.

**Source brief**: `docs/baby-log-book-idea.md`.

**Skills every session should consult**: `/grilling` and `/domain-modeling` by default; `/research` for research tickets; `/prototype` for prototype tickets; `/impeccable` for anything touching UI.

**Standing constraints** — settled while charting, do not re-litigate without saying so:

- **This is a distributable product, not one person's server.** An **AGPL** self-hosted Docker image that strangers pull and run behind whatever proxy they already have; a hosted version is a much-later maybe and out of scope. Settled by [Deployment shape](issues/12-deployment-shape.md) and it re-aims earlier tickets — the boot claim link and the Rescue Link CLI are a stranger's onboarding path, not the author's private recovery hatch. The governing rule: **anything the deployment needs done correctly, the app does — not the proxy, not the runbook.**
- **One Household per deployment** ([ADR-0009](../../docs/adr/0009-one-household-per-deployment.md)). No household selector, no multi-tenancy in v1.
- **Stack**: SvelteKit, `adapter-node`, PWA. SQLite in a single Docker container, DB on a mounted volume. No separate DB process. **The server renders no UI** — one prerendered shell, precached, everything drawn client-side from the replica ([ADR-0012](../../docs/adr/0012-the-app-is-a-precached-shell.md)); `adapter-node` serves the sync and claim endpoints and that shell, nothing else.
- **CSS**: pure CSS with custom properties. **Not** utility-class-heavy — Tailwind and friends are excluded by the brief. A lightweight component framework is wanted. Settled as Pico v2 + Open Props tokens; the visual system itself is [`design/tokens.css`](design/tokens.css) and is the only place a colour, radius or font size may be named.
- **Users**: parents *and* grandparents. Per-person accounts joining a **household**; roles are **parent** (manages household, babies, invites; can delete) and **caregiver** (logs and views). Every entry records who logged it, and attribution is shown in the timeline.
- **Corrections**: anyone in the household may correct anyone's entry; edits keep a visible history. Restrictions apply to deleting and to household management, not to fixing a number.
- **Offline**: offline-first for writes, with the household's **entire** log replicated locally. Client-generated UUIDs, last-write-wins on edits. Sessions long-lived so a device works offline with no server contact; no device PIN in v1.
- **Every member sees everything.** No per-member visibility scoping — replication is uniform across the household. Revocation stops *future* fetches; data already on a device stays there. Accepted deliberately.
- **The day boundary is a configured time**, set in schedule settings — not midnight, and not derived from the first feed. A 01:30 feed belongs to the previous day.
- **Live sessions**: feeds and sleeps are started and stopped as live timers (one-thumb operation is the design constraint), with manual after-the-fact entry and correction always available. Duplicate open sessions are reconciled by a **merge rule at sync time**, not by server-side validation.
- **Multi-baby**: in the data model from day one; the v1 UI hides the selector until there is more than one baby.
- **Languages**: EN, DE, RO, per-user preference. Metric units only.
- **Phasing**: v1 = logging + timeline + light schedules (elapsed-vs-target on screen) + daily/weekly stats + CSV export + milestones (added by [Stats and CSV export](issues/10-stats-and-export.md), because milestones are unrepeatable and a v2 that adds them cannot recover the ones v1 missed). v2 = push notifications. v3 = growth charts, monthly/yearly stats.
- **Export**: manual download of all household data as CSV.

**Tracker note**: research findings are captured under `.scratch/baby-log-book/research/` on the working branch, not on throwaway `research/<name>` branches — the tracker itself lives in the repo, so a branch would hide the findings from the map.

**Where things live**: [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md) is the vocabulary, [`docs/adr/`](../../docs/adr/) the decision records, [`spec.md`](spec.md) the assembled shape. A term is defined in exactly one of them.

## Decisions so far

<!-- one line per resolved ticket: gist + link -->

- [Prototype: the one-thumb logging screen](issues/08-logging-ux-prototype.md) — the timeline is the screen, with one FAB for logging and a sticky header carrying "since last feed" and "next due". Overdue shifts colour rather than nagging. [Prototype](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7a11190e-e5dc-43bd-b568-a6083c901f09), variant D.
- [Logging interactions: what the FAB opens, and stale sleep recovery](issues/16-logging-interactions.md) — the FAB **expands into a fan of direct actions**, so a nappy logs with no sheet and no confirm; undo over confirm, decided explicitly. A forgotten sleep gets a **passive banner** — **the app never writes data nobody entered**, which rejects auto-closing and sets a precedent for [Bottle freshness](issues/15-bottle-freshness.md). The picker defaults to her usual wake time, not now.
- [Domain model and entry taxonomy](issues/05-domain-model.md) — six entry types in one `entries` table with a JSON payload; append-only revisions and permanent soft deletes; a Live Session is just an entry with no end. Feeds are anchored by their **start** and their end is optional; a Sleep's end is essential. Glossary at [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md), decisions in [ADR-0001](../../docs/adr/0001-single-entries-table.md) and [ADR-0002](../../docs/adr/0002-append-only-revisions.md).
- [Local-first sync engine: build or buy?](issues/01-local-first-sync-engine.md) — **build**: hand-rolled sync on Dexie, implementing RxDB's documented pull/push/checkpoint contract, ~500–800 lines. "No Postgres, no separate service" eliminates Zero, PowerSync and ElectricSQL outright; only RxDB and TinyBase survive, and neither sells us the one rule we need — the duplicate-open-session merge is cross-document business logic that costs the same either way. RxDB is the named fallback (same server endpoints), TinyBase the pick if UI reactivity decides it. Findings: [`research/local-first-sync-engine.md`](research/local-first-sync-engine.md).
- [Lightweight CSS framework for the UI](issues/02-css-framework.md) — **Pico CSS v2 for components + Open Props for tokens + ~200 lines of our own.** Pico's dark mode is a pure custom-property swap with no JS, and it carries only 16 class selectors in 11.4 KB. Open Props is a token layer only — it ships no components. Franken UI is Tailwind in disguise and is out; Open Props UI is the named exit if Pico's silent `main` stays silent. Findings: [`research/css-framework.md`](research/css-framework.md).
- [SQLite driver and migration tooling](issues/03-sqlite-driver-and-migrations.md) — **`better-sqlite3` 13.x + plain SQL behind a thin typed helper + a hand-rolled boot-time migration runner.** The ticket's premise is overturned: better-sqlite3 13.0.2+ ships prebuilt Node-API binaries (Alpine included) and no longer compiles on install, so there is nothing to dodge. `node:sqlite` is still RC in Node 26 and no query layer supports it. Kysely's SQLite migrator runs outside a transaction; one table and ~10 queries do not pay for Drizzle. Findings: [`research/sqlite-driver-and-migrations.md`](research/sqlite-driver-and-migrations.md).
- [i18n for EN, DE and RO](issues/04-i18n-approach.md) — **Paraglide JS 2**, catalogues as plain JSON in git. Offline falls out of the architecture: messages compile to ESM at build time and SvelteKit's worker already precaches them. Ship all three locales — the bundle-vs-offline-switching tension is illusory. `sveltekit-i18n` has no plural modifier at all; `svelte-i18n`'s own SSR guide leaks language across requests. Findings: [`research/i18n-approach.md`](research/i18n-approach.md).
- [Sync protocol and conflict semantics](issues/06-sync-protocol.md) — sync moves an **append-only log of field-level revisions**, not rows, which makes it **insert-only**: idempotent by construction, order-independent, and impossible for a stale client to clobber a field it doesn't know. One log carries entries, foods, babies, members and settings. The **cursor and the merge key are different clocks** — server `seq` for the feed, corrected client clock for LWW — and conflating either loses data. Any two open sessions for one Baby are a contradiction, so the merge needs no time window; it never invents an end time. The user never sees a conflict. [ADR-0003](../../docs/adr/0003-revisions-are-the-sync-unit.md), [ADR-0004](../../docs/adr/0004-cursor-is-not-the-merge-key.md).
- [Accounts, households and invites](issues/07-accounts-and-invites.md) — **no passwords.** One primitive, a single-use **Claim Link**, covers all three ways in: an **Invite** a Parent sends (7 days, carries name and role), a **Rescue Link** minted from the container for the lost phone, and first-run bootstrap. It must be claimed by a **POST behind a button** — a link that claims on GET is burnt by WhatsApp's preview bot before it arrives. Sessions never expire; revocation is the control, and the token sits in an HttpOnly cookie. `device_id` outlives the session and is **never** proof of identity. Multiple Parents, last Parent undeletable. Removal is a state, not a deletion, so attribution survives forever. [ADR-0005](../../docs/adr/0005-claim-links-instead-of-passwords.md).
- [Schedules in v1: targets and elapsed-vs-target](issues/09-schedules-v1.md) — a schedule is **one number per activity per Baby and no machinery**: intervals only, with two anchors — Feed Interval from the previous feed's start, **Wake Window from the last sleep's end**. Targets are **stated, never learned** ([ADR-0006](../../docs/adr/0006-targets-are-stated-not-learned.md)) and nothing is ever materialised. Day Start defaults to **05:00**, is per Household, and re-buckets the past when changed; the **Night Sleep is the one that crosses it**, which closes a gap in `CONTEXT.md`. The stale-sleep threshold is a hidden age-banded ceiling plus a **Meal** contradiction — a *Feed* cannot be the signal, because a [Sleep Feed](../../CONTEXT.md) is normal and nightly, which also corrects [Sync protocol](issues/06-sync-protocol.md) to merge sessions **per kind**, and gives the FAB a *Feed while asleep* action.
- [Stats and CSV export](issues/10-stats-and-export.md) — **stats is a trend screen and only that**: four cards over a **rolling seven days plus today**, where today is drawn in-progress and kept out of the delta, because a half-finished day in the comparison would report every morning that things are getting worse. Cards appear only when their entry type has data, so age-appropriateness is free. Client-side, nothing materialised, bars hand-rolled. The **Export is an escape hatch, not a backup** ([ADR-0007](../../docs/adr/0007-export-is-an-escape-hatch.md)) — everything, always, no options, as a zip of per-type CSVs, a shape *forced* by a Meal holding several Foods. Two spin-offs: **Milestones come into scope for v1** and history turns out to be timeline filtering, not a stats tab.
- [Prototype: visual design direction](issues/11-visual-design-direction.md) — **D1 "Instrument", with a bottom tab bar**: one hue, and it belongs to *actions* — entry types are told apart by glyph and label, and every `--t-*` token resolves to `--ink-2` so the token layer enforces it. Overdue is the number adopting the brand colour, which makes *the app does not nag* structurally impossible rather than merely intended. The big win came from re-asking the night question: **the appearance follows the clock, and the clock can only ever make it darker** ([ADR-0008](../../docs/adr/0008-appearance-follows-the-clock.md)) — deep night from 23:00 to Day Start, no light mode after 19:00 whatever the phone says. Costs the no-JS theming the CSS research prized. Pico survives almost intact: **63 of its 149 tokens mapped**, one line fighting it, no `!important`. Assets: [prototype](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/8d2f7632-bdb5-4c5d-894f-028517aac47c), [`design/tokens.css`](design/tokens.css), [`design/appearance.js`](design/appearance.js).
- [Deployment shape: one container on your server](issues/12-deployment-shape.md) — the ticket's premise is overturned: it isn't *your* server, it's **an AGPL image strangers pull**, so every "configure your proxy to do X" answer became a support burden and moved into the app. Rate limiting came off the proxy (correcting [Accounts](issues/07-accounts-and-invites.md)); `ORIGIN` is required and the container **refuses to boot without it**, because a wrong origin mints dead Claim Links *silently*; the signing key lives in the volume, not an env var, since a redeploy that forgets it signs out every Device at once. Migrations refuse-to-start on failure — read-only is a trap that lets Devices queue pushes nobody will accept — and **a backup is taken before every migration**, which is the only thing that makes "roll back to the previous tag" real. One Household per deployment, multi-arch, no HTTP admin endpoint. [ADR-0009](../../docs/adr/0009-one-household-per-deployment.md).
- [Timezones and travel](issues/17-timezones-and-travel.md) — **an instant is the only thing the app ever compares; a zone is a lens applied at display time** ([ADR-0010](../../docs/adr/0010-instants-are-stored-the-zone-is-a-lens.md)). `Occurred At` stores a UTC instant plus the **IANA zone id** the Device was in, never a bare offset — an offset cannot re-derive a Day Start — and **nothing in v1 reads that zone**, captured only because it is unrecoverable later, the Milestones argument again. One **Household Zone** is the single bucketing lens, one value not a history, **suggested after 48h in a new zone but never applied**. The headline is that [ADR-0008](../../docs/adr/0008-appearance-follows-the-clock.md)'s open question resolves to **divergence: the hour travels, the zone does not** — appearance reads the Device's own clock, or Oma gets a white screen in a dark bedroom in Bucharest. Durations are elapsed real time, so a Sleep across spring-forward is 7h. Confirms a zone change is not skew under [ADR-0004](../../docs/adr/0004-cursor-is-not-the-merge-key.md), and states the invariant that keeps it so.
- [Milestones as an entry type](issues/18-milestones-entry-type.md) — the seventh type costs one fan row, one glyph and one string; everything that could have made it expensive was rejected. **A Milestone Name is written, not chosen** ([ADR-0011](../../docs/adr/0011-milestone-names-are-written-not-chosen.md)): a free-text string with Food's combobox but **not Food's catalogue** — the suggestion list is *derived* from the Milestones themselves, because a name you type once in your life never earns a mutable entity, and a derived list cannot drift. A date-only field was rejected for breaking [ADR-0010](../../docs/adr/0010-instants-are-stored-the-zone-is-a-lens.md)'s one invariant; the instant stays and **the timeline just shows no clock time**, with back-dated Milestones landing at their day's head. It keeps the universal Note, because the anti-baby-book boundary is held by what is *absent*, not by amputating a field all six siblings have. Two corrections outward: **the Stats card rule is restated as *cards appear for entry types that have a rate***, so it stays at four, and the glyph is a **flag**.
- [Timeline filtering and food history](issues/19-timeline-filtering.md) — **the filter takes the header's place.** D1 spends its only hue on actions, so "this is filtered" had to be said without colour, and the winning answer says it with the *largest element on the screen*: the live hero figure is gone and inverted ink stands where it was. That single choice also settles the header (the elapsed-vs-target figures are about now, and no version of them is correct in a historical view, so they go) and the **Food detail view, which turns out to be a pre-filtered timeline and not a screen** — no second surface, and the reaction notes come along because they *are* the rows. Milestone is an ordinary facet, which closes [Milestones](issues/18-milestones-entry-type.md)'s handoff. Choosing it left the hard question open rather than closing it, because it is the one variant that keeps the FAB live: resolved as **logging clears the filter**, which extends [Logging interactions](issues/16-logging-interactions.md)'s rule to its mirror image — *the app never hides data somebody just entered*. A filter is a lookup, not a setting: it survives a tab switch, never a cold start. All five facets earn v1, the date one as three preset chips and no picker. [Prototype](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/bc155120-87ce-433a-bb17-964394c79f81), variant A.
- [PWA install and update strategy](issues/20-pwa-update-strategy.md) — **the app never reloads a screen nobody asked it to reload**, the mirror of [Logging interactions](issues/16-logging-interactions.md)'s rule: no prompt, no toast, no badge for an ordinary version, which installs and waits and takes over only at a moment indistinguishable from a cold launch — and **a running Live Session defers it**, closing the 3am hole with [Schedules in v1](issues/09-schedules-v1.md)'s stale ceiling and no new state. Detection piggybacks on sync; nothing else polls. "What does the user see while it happens" turns out to have **no answer** — the worker precaches before it waits, so there is no moment to show. Two spin-offs went deeper than the ticket: the caching question was really **does the server render any UI at all** — no, one precached shell ([ADR-0012](../../docs/adr/0012-the-app-is-a-precached-shell.md)), which also caught a hole in [ADR-0008](../../docs/adr/0008-appearance-follows-the-clock.md) nothing else would have found, since the **OS launch screen paints before any of our code exists** and its colour lives in a JSON file that cannot read a clock. And the outbox question exposed an invariant worth more than it: **the replica is a cache, the outbox is data** ([ADR-0013](../../docs/adr/0013-the-replica-is-a-cache-the-outbox-is-data.md)) — so an update never waits for it, and a new client reading an old client's outbox is a contract. Install is not cosmetic for the same reason. Third instance of a Device-scoped preference, so **Device Setting** is now a term in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md); v1 also ships the **mirror skew** — a Device ahead of a rolled-back server — because it is impossible to retrofit onto a fleet already stuck.
- [Assemble the spec and ADRs](issues/13-assemble-the-spec.md) — **the destination**: [`spec.md`](spec.md), eleven sections, duplicating neither the glossary nor the ADRs. Mostly folding rather than writing, so the real work was **reconciling the four corrections later tickets made to earlier ones** and stating which reading wins — per-kind Session Merge, rate limiting in the app not the proxy, stats cards for entry types with a *rate*, one Household per Member in v1. The five rules the map kept re-deriving from different directions are promoted to invariants on page one. Two loose ends closed rather than reported: [Deployment shape](issues/12-deployment-shape.md)'s stranger-facing wording for the boot link and `babylog rescue`, and the `--t-milestone` token [Milestones](issues/18-milestones-entry-type.md) asked for and never got.
- [Bottle freshness](issues/15-bottle-freshness.md) — **reopened 2026-08-18 and built**, after the scope call that closed it. Exactly the shape the grilling had reached: a **third Target** (`bottle` / `bottle_start`), seeded at 1h, edited per Baby beside the Feed Interval and the Wake Window — no new machinery, no Bottle entity, no per-contents thresholds, and **no verdict** ([ADR-0016](../../docs/adr/0016-the-bottle-life-is-a-target-not-a-verdict.md)). The countdown lives on the row of a bottle that is still open, not in the header, because a Combined Feed can have two open at once. The argument that closed the ticket is unanswered and now carried in the UI instead: the clock starts at the Feed, so it always reads *younger* than the milk, and Settings says so beside the field. One departure from the closed shape — the Target is **seeded rather than blank**, at the user's request, so the countdown works without anyone typing a number. First `PROTOCOL_VERSION` bump the project has had, because an old client coerces the unknown activity to `feed` and would overwrite the record.
- [What may a caregiver see?](issues/14-caregiver-visibility-scope.md) — everything. No visibility scoping; replication stays uniform and simple. Revocation is a server-side gate on future syncs, and data already fetched onto a device is accepted as unrecoverable.
- [Feeding defaults](issues/21-feeding-defaults.md) — the feed sheet opens the way this Device says feeds usually happen: a **Feeding default Device Setting** (Breast / Bottle · breast milk / Bottle · formula), stated in Settings and never learned, per §9.4's carve-out — mum breastfeeds, Oma bottles. Contents became a two-button segmented row directly under the mode switch; `other` stays a domain value editable in the entry sheet but is no longer a logging-time option.
- [Bottle volumes: netto vs. brutto](issues/22-bottle-netto-brutto.md) — **ADR-0015 reversed by [ADR-0018](../../docs/adr/0018-a-bottle-records-the-intake.md)**: a Bottle stores one amount, the **Intake**, and the leftover became a subtraction affordance that applies once, clamps at zero, clears itself and is never stored. The old derivation survives as the reading lens for legacy rows, so no migration and no protocol bump. Formula presets speak water and write milk (60→70 … 270→305, hardcoded); the export collapses to one `intake_ml` column, an accepted loss the revision log covers.
- [Tummy time as an entry type](issues/25-tummy-time.md) — **the eighth type and the fourth Live Session**, requested directly rather than reached by grilling. A Sleep without the payload: two ends, an empty payload, and everything the timeline draws for a running session following from `SESSION_TYPES` membership — which is why the change grew one predicate, `isSession()`, in place of the three inline type lists three components were each keeping. The decision worth the ADR is the one it refuses: tummy time's end is the whole point, the shape §3.3 says earns a stale rescue, and **it gets no banner anyway** ([ADR-0027](../../docs/adr/0027-tummy-time-ends-in-the-fan.md)) — the stale-Sleep machinery is aimed at hours in the dark with nobody watching, and a stretch is minutes in daylight with an adult over her. The fan carries the end instead, reflowing to *Off her tummy* and writing straight through. Accepted cost, stated rather than mitigated: a forgotten stop inflates the day's total and nothing corrects it. No merge ([ADR-0014](../../docs/adr/0014-only-sleeps-merge.md) unchanged: two open stretches are two rows, and merging would delete minutes) and no Target — it is reported, never scheduled. Fifth stats card, quarter-hour axis, moss 100° — the last hue the accent band and meal green left free.
- **A nappy is one form** ([ADR-0028](../../docs/adr/0028-a-nappy-is-one-form.md)) — Pee and Poop stop being two fan rows and become two toggles on one sheet, reversing [Logging interactions](issues/16-logging-interactions.md)'s straight-from-the-fan nappy. The height argument (seven rows had reached ~510px, ~560px with sub-lines) is the weaker half; the real one is that **two rows wrote two Entries**, so a nappy that held both was logged twice and inflated the day's count — *both* is not a third kind of nappy, it is the two booleans the payload has stored since v1. The form is also the first input the **consistency** has ever had, a field the model carried and the UI could not write, and the entry sheet grew the same three fields because a value the app can write but never fix would be the one exception to corrections being first-class. Cost, paid deliberately: the most frequent action loses the shortest path, and undo-not-confirm goes with it since the form is the confirm. An in-place reflow to *Pee · Poop · Back* was tried first and abandoned — it solved the height and left both real problems standing.

## Not yet specified

- **One entry type for the nappy, the potty and the toilet** — [ticket 26](issues/26-one-type-for-nappy-potty-toilet.md), **resolved**. *Nappy* names the receptacle, so it goes wrong the day a Baby uses a potty, and *Diaper* fails on the same day in a different dialect. The type is now **Pee & poop** / **Pipi & Kaka** / **Pipi & caca** — the two facts it holds, in each language the same words the form's own toggles use, naming no receptacle in any of the three (asserted by test) — and the receptacle became a nullable `where` (nappy / potty / toilet) inside the form. It is the **one prefilled field on a form whose rule is that nothing is prefilled**, and the distinction that makes that consistent is worth keeping: *pee and poop are facts about this event; the receptacle is a fact about this phase*, so it opens on a Device Setting shaped like the [Feeding default](issues/21-feeding-defaults.md). Never backfilled and the silence never read as a nappy — an old row opens with nothing selected and exports an empty column. The stored discriminator and `nappies.csv` stayed put: a UI name is not a column. Notably, it cost **no fan row** — [ticket 27](issues/27-the-fan-is-out-of-room.md)'s constraint doing its job for the first time.
- **The fan is out of room** — [ticket 27](issues/27-the-fan-is-out-of-room.md), open. Six rows is ~455px and about the limit; two entry types arrived in a fortnight and the next one decides the question by force. Recommendation is a **two-column grid plus a *More* sheet** — roughly double the capacity with no common action getting deeper — with the rare types leaving the fan as cheap relief. The lesson [ADR-0028](../../docs/adr/0028-a-nappy-is-one-form.md) paid for is written into the ticket: **a level down is not capacity, it is a tax**, and it lands on exactly the actions the fan exists to protect.

- **v2 push notifications** — approach and feasibility, especially web push on iOS PWAs. Now largely pinned by [Schedules in v1](issues/09-schedules-v1.md): the schedule model needs **no schema change** for v2, the due-instant computation lives in one shared module the notifier will reuse, preferences will be Device-scoped, and the forgotten-sleep case comes free from the stale-sleep rules. What remains is genuinely a delivery question — iOS PWA web push, permission prompts, and what a notification does when the Device has been offline for a day. Narrowed again by [PWA install and update strategy](issues/20-pwa-update-strategy.md): **iOS delivers web push only to installed PWAs**, so v1's install nudge is the precondition for v2 on an iPhone and the feasibility question is now *how many Devices actually installed*, not whether the platform allows it. Device-scoped preferences are no longer a guess either — **Device Setting** is a named term with a rule (it never enters the sync log), so the preference has a shelf to sit on.
- **Growth charts and WHO percentile curves** (v3) — data source, licensing, rendering.
- **Monthly/yearly stats presentation** (v3) — what a year of a baby's life should actually show. Partly pinned by [Stats and CSV export](issues/10-stats-and-export.md): v1 is a fixed rolling week with **no navigation to earlier periods at all**, so v3 inherits the whole question of browsing history — a period picker, how far back is worth rendering, and whether a year of days is still bars. The four cards and the today-is-excluded-from-the-delta rule are the v1 shape it either extends or breaks from deliberately. Narrowed by [Timeline filtering](issues/19-timeline-filtering.md): *browsing* history is now answered — the timeline filters, and the date facet ships as three preset chips. What v3 still owns is **aggregating** over a period, and it inherits a decision it may not want, since the timeline's period is a preset and a year view probably needs a real range.
- **Drawing travel in the time it actually happened** — a fortnight abroad rendered in the zone each Entry was logged in, rather than re-read through whatever the Household Zone is today. Deferred by [Timezones and travel](issues/17-timezones-and-travel.md), which took the one step that cannot be taken retroactively: the **Recording Zone is captured from v1** even though nothing reads it, so this arrives without a migration. What is genuinely open is presentation, not storage — day buckets that span two zones, and whether printed times inside one bucket can stop being monotonic without becoming unreadable, which is the objection that killed the naive version.
- **Optional device PIN / biometric lock** — deferred to v2 (see Standing constraints). [Accounts](issues/07-accounts-and-invites.md) raised the stakes rather than changed the decision: with no password and no session expiry, an unlocked phone that is found is a signed-in Household. For v1 the answer is the phone's own lock screen and a Parent revoking the Device.

## Out of scope

- **Health events** — medication, temperature, illness, doctor visits. Not in the brief; a separate domain with its own model.
- **Photos and diary entries** — this is a log, not a baby book. Photos are an attachment story (storage, sync payload size, thumbnails) and diary entries are prose. **Milestones were bundled into this line and have been split back out** — a Milestone is a point in time with a label, which is what an Entry already is, so it is in scope and in v1. See [Stats and CSV export](issues/10-stats-and-export.md) and [Milestones as an entry type](issues/18-milestones-entry-type.md). The boundary that keeps this line shut: a Milestone is one line of text, no photo, no attachment, no prose. Now resolved, and the boundary turned out to be held by **what is absent** rather than by any restriction on the Milestone itself — it keeps the universal Note like every other Entry, and what does the enforcing is a single-line input with no photo, no attachment, no rich text and no screen of its own.
- **Multi-household sharing** — separated parents, public share links, exporting a baby to another household.
- **Nutrition data and allergen taxonomies** — solids tracking stops at a household-built food catalogue with first-exposure and a free-text reaction note.
- **Native app store builds** — PWA only.
- **A hosted / multi-tenant cloud version** — a possible much-later product for people who don't want to self-host, and a different deployment mode rather than a v1 schema tax. Ruled out by [Deployment shape](issues/12-deployment-shape.md); the door isn't nailed shut, since every row already carries a Household boundary.
- **Imperial units.**
- ~~**Milk freshness**~~ — **reopened 2026-08-18 and built**, as the third Target the grilling had settled on before the scope call. See the Decisions-so-far entry above and [Bottle freshness](issues/15-bottle-freshness.md). What stays out of scope is narrower than the old line: **no Bottle entity** (no prepared / offered / refrigerated lifecycle), **no preparation time**, **no per-contents thresholds**, and **no verdict** — the app counts against a number a Member typed and never says whether the milk is safe.
