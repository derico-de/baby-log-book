# Bottle volumes: netto vs. brutto for formula

Type: task
Category: enhancement
Status: resolved

## Request (from the dev, 2026-08-18)

> Allow netto/brutto values for bottle quantities. Formula is 150ml water +
> powder > final value is 170ml. So in case you want to count the final values
> the quantities need to be: 70ml, 100ml, 135ml, 170ml, 200ml, and so on.

## Decisions (triage, 2026-08-18)

Grilling reshaped this from a preset tweak into a domain change, decided
explicitly by the maintainer:

- **ADR-0015 is reversed** — a Bottle Feed now records one amount, the
  **Intake**. [ADR-0018](../../../docs/adr/0018-a-bottle-records-the-intake.md)
  supersedes it; `CONTEXT.md` and spec §3.2/§3.4/§8.2 are updated. The
  leftover is a subtraction affordance that updates the Intake field and is
  never stored; the old offered/leftover derivation survives as the reading
  rule for legacy rows, so there is no migration and no protocol bump.
- **Two personas, one field.** Formula flow: tap a water-measure preset →
  the derived final volume lands in the Intake field; a leftover entered at
  the end subtracts in place. Breast-milk-bottle flow: type the intake
  directly (or calculate it yourself). Either way the field is the fact, and
  log/stats/export show only it.
- **Formula presets are hardcoded, water-primary.** Buttons show the water
  measure big with the final volume as a hint (60→70, 90→100, 120→135,
  150→170, 180→200, 210→235, 240→270, 270→305); tapping writes the *final*
  value. Breast-milk presets stay the plain list. No setting, no
  configurable factor — the free field covers brand mismatches.
- **Accepted loss, flagged**: the export drops the offered/leftover pair for
  legacy rows in favour of a single `intake_ml` column; the revision log
  keeps the originals permanently (ADR-0002).

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** Bottle Feeds record one Intake figure; leftover becomes a
subtract-and-clear affordance; formula presets speak water measures and write
the derived final volume. Implements ADR-0018 (read it first — it is the
contract this brief serializes).

**Current behavior:**
A bottle payload stores `volume_ml` (what was offered) beside a nullable
`leftover_ml`; what she drank is derived by a clamped-subtraction helper and
never stored. The feed sheet offers one hardcoded quick-amount list
(60…270 ml) regardless of contents, labels the field "Volume", and shows the
derived figure as a note line. The timeline renders "130 ml of 170" when a
leftover exists, stats total the derived figure, and the CSV export carries
`volume_ml`, `leftover_ml` and `taken_ml`.

**Desired behavior:**

- Every new bottle write stores the Intake in `volume_ml` and never sets a
  leftover. `leftover_ml` remains in the payload type, read-only, for rows
  written before the change.
- One reading lens serves both eras: legacy rows read as
  `volume − leftover` clamped at zero (null leftover = whole bottle), new
  rows pass the Intake through. Renaming the existing helper to say "intake"
  is welcome; having two helpers is not.
- The bottle amount field is labelled **Intake** (EN/DE/RO). Presets depend
  on contents: breast milk (and legacy `other`) keeps today's plain list,
  written directly; formula shows the eight water-primary buttons from the
  decisions above, each writing its **final** volume into the field.
- The leftover input — in both the logging sheet and the entry edit sheet —
  becomes the affordance: confirming a value (blur or enter) subtracts it
  from the Intake field once, clamped at zero, then clears itself. Nothing
  is stored. Entering 40 then 10 against 170 leaves 120.
- Editing a legacy row shows the derived Intake as its amount; saving an
  amount change writes the new Intake and explicitly nulls the stored
  leftover (a real field write, so last-write-wins converges). Untouched
  legacy rows keep their pair.
- The timeline shows the Intake figure only; the "X of Y" form is retired
  for all rows.
- The bottle CSV export carries a single `intake_ml` column (the lens value)
  in place of `volume_ml`, `leftover_ml` and `taken_ml`.
- Stats totals are numerically unchanged (same lens).
- No `PROTOCOL_VERSION` change — ADR-0018 records why both write shapes read
  correctly on both client generations.

**Key interfaces:**

- `BottleFeedPayload` — `volume_ml` semantics become Intake; `leftover_ml`
  frozen for legacy reads.
- The taken/intake derivation helper — becomes the single reading lens for
  timeline, stats and export.
- The feed sheet's quick-amount list — split by contents; the formula
  water→final table lives beside it as a hardcoded constant.
- Export headers for the bottle-feed section.
- Message catalogue — new/changed labels for Intake, the leftover affordance
  and the formula preset hint, in EN, DE and RO.

**Acceptance criteria:**

- [ ] Saving a new bottle stores the field value as `volume_ml` with no
      leftover, whatever the contents.
- [ ] With formula selected, the presets read 60/90/120/150/180/210/240/270
      with →70/→100/→135/→170/→200/→235/→270/→305 hints, and tapping [150]
      puts 170 in the Intake field; breast-milk presets behave as today.
- [ ] Confirming leftover 40 against Intake 170 updates the field to 130 and
      clears the input, in both sheets; a subsequent 10 yields 120; a
      leftover larger than the Intake clamps to 0.
- [ ] A legacy row (volume 170, leftover 40) shows 130 in timeline, stats
      and export; changing its amount in the edit sheet writes the new
      Intake and nulls the leftover in the revision.
- [ ] No timeline row renders the "X of Y" form.
- [ ] The export's bottle section has an `intake_ml` column and no
      `volume_ml`/`leftover_ml`/`taken_ml`; legacy rows export their derived
      intake.
- [ ] `PROTOCOL_VERSION` is unchanged.
- [ ] Unit tests cover the lens across both eras, the affordance arithmetic
      (apply-once, clamp), the formula preset mapping, and the export
      column; the existing suites pass.
- [ ] All touched labels exist in EN, DE and RO.

**Out of scope:**

- Configurable brand tables, factors, or any Settings surface for this.
- A Bottle entity, preparation time, or per-contents freshness (ADR-0016/17
  territory).
- Rewriting stored legacy revisions in bulk — conversion happens only when a
  row is edited.
- Ticket 21 (feeding defaults and the contents button row). Independent: this
  works against the current contents `<select>` too; if 21 lands first the
  presets simply react to its button row.
- Breast-feed (duration) presets and the meal flow.

## Answer

Built per ADR-0018. `takenMl` became `intakeMl`, the single reading lens for
both eras (new rows pass through; legacy rows read volume − leftover, clamped);
timeline, stats and export all read through it and the "X of Y" form is gone.
New bottle writes store the Intake in `volume_ml` and never set a leftover
(`logBottleFeed` lost its `leftoverMl` parameter). The leftover input in both
sheets is now `subtractLeftover` — confirm on blur/enter, subtract once,
clamp at zero, clear itself, store nothing. Formula presets are the hardcoded
water-primary table (60→70 … 270→305) writing the final volume; breast-milk
presets unchanged. Editing a legacy row shows the derived Intake and an amount
change writes the new Intake plus an explicit `leftover_ml: null`. The export
carries one `intake_ml` column. `PROTOCOL_VERSION` untouched. Labels in
EN/DE/RO; tests across `entries.test.ts`, `export.test.ts`,
`feed-sheet.test.ts`, `entry-sheet.test.ts` and `render.test.ts`.
