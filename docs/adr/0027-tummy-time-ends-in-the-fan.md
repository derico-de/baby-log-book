# Tummy time ends in the fan, not in a banner

Tummy Time is the eighth Entry type and the fourth Live Session, and like a Sleep its **end is the whole point** — nobody asks when tummy time started, they ask how long it lasted today. That is the shape [spec §3.3](../../.scratch/baby-log-book/spec.md) says earns a Stale Session rescue: a Sleep left running has destroyed the record it existed to make, so it gets a banner. **Tummy Time gets no banner, and no Session Merge either.** What it gets instead is a fan that reflows: while a stretch runs, the tummy row stops offering to start one and offers *Off her tummy*, which ends it at the instant it is pressed.

The banner was rejected because the stale-Sleep machinery is not free — a ceiling seeded from the Baby's age, a device-local acknowledgement per session, a *Still asleep* clock reset, and a whole surface competing for the top of the timeline — and it is aimed at a session measured in hours that runs through the night while everyone is asleep. Tummy time is minutes long, happens in daylight, and the person who put her down is standing over her. A second banner would be the loudest possible answer to the quietest version of the problem, and two banners racing for the same slot is a UI question nobody needs to answer.

Session Merge is refused for [ADR-0014](0014-only-sleeps-merge.md)'s reason read forwards: the merge rests on a contradiction — a Baby cannot be asleep twice — and two open stretches of tummy time are not a contradiction, only two people logging the same minutes. Merging them would tombstone one, and the failure would be the Feed failure again: a duration silently leaving the day's total. Two rows on the timeline, one tap to delete, is the visible answer.

## Consequences

- **A forgotten stop inflates the day's tummy-time total, and nothing corrects it.** This is the accepted cost, and it is the one place Tummy Time is weaker than Sleep. The mitigations are all visible ones: the row carries *running* and a Stop button, the fan says *Off her tummy* from wherever the thumb already is, and the entry sheet corrects the end like any other field. The app still writes no end nobody entered — ADR-0017's server-authored end stands alone, and it stands on a Target a Member typed.
- **`SESSION_TYPES` is the seam, so the timeline came free.** Running rows, the live pill, the Stop button and the duration line all ask `isSession()` rather than naming three types inline, which is why the fourth type reached every one of them at once.
- **No Target, so no header column.** Tummy Time is reported, never scheduled: there is nothing to be overdue against, the live grid stays the two columns it was, and the running stretch is read from the replica rather than folded into the header.
- **The stats card is a duration card with a quarter-hour axis.** The rule that cards appear for entry types with a rate ([issue 18](../../.scratch/baby-log-book/issues/18-milestones-entry-type.md)) admits it: minutes per day is a rate, and it is the figure the whole feature exists to produce. An hourly axis would draw every honest day as a stub, so the axis unit is 15 minutes.
- **One hue was left, and it is spent.** Moss 100° is the last gap between the accent band's upper edge and Meal's green ([ADR-0026](0026-entry-types-get-their-own-colour.md)). A ninth entry type will have to reopen the palette rather than pick a leftover.
