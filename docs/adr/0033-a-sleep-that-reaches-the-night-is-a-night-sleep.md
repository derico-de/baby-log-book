# A Sleep that reaches the Night is a Night Sleep

A Sleep is a **Night Sleep** when it crosses the Day Start **or** when it reaches into the Household's [Night Period](0032-the-night-period-ends-at-the-day-start.md); every other Sleep is a Nap. This overturns the accepted cost spec §7.2 wrote down: the 19:00 bedtime that collapses at 23:00 was filed as a Nap, and it is not one.

§7.2 chose the crossing rule because *"a single hour cannot classify both ends of a night"* and because the alternative meant **a Night Start setting nobody wants to configure**. The second half of that argument has expired — [ADR-0031](0031-a-notice-is-a-target-reached-and-the-household-states-the-offset.md) and [ADR-0032](0032-the-night-period-ends-at-the-day-start.md) brought the hour in for the Feed Interval, for a reason that had nothing to do with Sleep, and a Household that has stated when its night begins has already said the thing the classification needed. Reading that hour twice is not a second setting; ignoring it while a bedtime is filed under Naps is the app knowing and not saying.

The first half never expired, and this does not contradict it: **one hour still cannot classify both ends**, which is why the Night Period is one hour that ends at the Day Start. The crossing rule keeps the morning end; the new clause only adds the evening the crossing rule could not see.

## Consequences

- **Two rules over one night, and they cannot disagree.** The Night Period ends at the Day Start ([ADR-0032](0032-the-night-period-ends-at-the-day-start.md)), so a Sleep that reaches the night and comes out the other side has crossed the Day Start and was a Night Sleep already. The added clause is strictly additive: it can turn a Nap into a Night Sleep and never the reverse.
- **Nothing changes for a Household that states no Night Period.** `night` is null, the new clause is false, and §7.2's crossing rule stands alone — the same quiet default ADR-0032 arrived with.
- **The stated hour now has a second reader, and it is not a Target.** ADR-0032's *Feeds only* bullet governs **due instants**, and still does: `dueInstant` is untouched, the Wake Window and the Bottle Life still say nothing about the hour. What is new is that the Night Period is no longer read only by the Feed. A Household that moves the hour moves both readings at once, which is the point of it being one fact.
- **A running bedtime turns as the evening reaches the hour.** A Sleep with no end is classified against `now`, so the timeline row says *Nap* at 19:30 and *Night Sleep* at 20:00. That is the same live reclassification the crossing rule already had at the Day Start, moved to an hour a parent will actually be awake for.
- **The small-hours stretch is a Night Sleep too.** She goes back down at 01:00 and is up at 03:00: inside the night from end to end, nowhere near the Day Start, and under the old rule a Nap. Naming it a Nap was the same error as the collapsed bedtime, and it fixes itself here.
- **The stale-Sleep picker skips a wake that lands in the night.** `usualWakeInstant` opens on the lower median of recent Night Sleep *ends* (spec §8.6). A collapsed bedtime ending at 22:00 is now a Night Sleep, and its hour would drag that median away from the morning it exists to find — so a Night Sleep that ended inside the Night is not counted as a wake. The morning end of the night is still the only thing that median reads.
- **Derived everywhere, stored nowhere.** Nap-versus-Night stays a reading over the log: the timeline row, the Sleep card's split, and the export's `kind` column all pass through `classifySleep`, and changing the Night Period re-reads every Sleep ever logged. Nothing migrates, and no Entry records which it was.
