# A running Sleep fills toward the Nap Length, or the Day Start

While a Sleep runs, the header's sleep bar fills toward the end of what that Sleep is allowed, the way the Feed's bar fills toward the Feed's due instant. What it is allowed depends on what the Sleep is ([ADR-0033](0033-a-sleep-that-reaches-the-night-is-a-night-sleep.md)): a Nap runs against the **Nap Length**, a **fourth Target** per Baby measured from the start of the Sleep she is in, seeded at an hour and a half; a Night Sleep runs against the Day Start that closes it — the night the Household already keeps ([ADR-0032](0032-the-night-period-ends-at-the-day-start.md)), read once more rather than stated again. Before this the running Sleep's bar stood full and named the instant she went down, which told a parent nothing the figure above it did not.

The Nap Length is a Target and not a Household setting because it is a fact about one Baby, like the Wake Window beside it in Schedule settings, and because a Target is already the one shape the app has for *a duration plus the Entry it measures from* ([ADR-0006](0006-targets-are-stated-not-learned.md), [ADR-0016](0016-the-bottle-life-is-a-target-not-a-verdict.md)). The night, by contrast, is a Household's: two Babies in one Household keep one night.

## Consequences

- **The bar's vocabulary does not change.** The countdown at its near end, the clock face at its far end, the fill heading for it, and the one shift once the instant has passed (`due +30m ······ 15:30`). The state word above it (`asleep`) is what says the instant is a wake and not a feed. *Amended 2026-10-05: the label does change — a running Sleep's bar reads `wake up in 25m ······ 16:25`. `due` beside `asleep` read as something owed, and the instant is her wake-up; past it, `woke up due 30m ago`, still the one shift.* A Sleep past its allowance is not an event the app has an opinion about: no Notice is sent, and the stale-Sleep banner (spec §8.6) remains the backstop for a Sleep nobody stopped.
- **The Nap Length has no age table.** How long one nap runs varies more between two naps of the same afternoon than between two ages, so a table would claim a precision it does not have. One band, 1h30, and the number is the Household's to change — the same shape as the Bottle Life.
- **Seeded, so no migration and no null case.** New Babies get a `nap` Target with the other three. A Baby added before it existed has no row; `napTargetOf` folds one at display time from the seed, and nothing is written until a Parent changes it.
- **A Night Sleep's measure is the Day Start, which is the bucket boundary and not her wake time.** This is the cost. A bedtime at 19:30 fills toward 05:00 and reads as past its allowance from 05:00 until she wakes at 07:00; a re-settle at 03:40 after a night waking has an hour and twenty minutes, not an hour and a half. The alternative — her usual wake, the lower median the stale banner's picker already computes — would be a learned number driving a bar, which [ADR-0006](0006-targets-are-stated-not-learned.md) rules out, and a Household whose Day Start sits far from her wake time can move the Day Start. Revisit if the overdue morning bar turns out to nag.
- **A bedtime's bar turns as the evening reaches the stated hour.** The running Sleep is classified against `now`, so at 20:30 the 19:30 bedtime fills toward 21:00 as a Nap and at 21:00 it is a Night Sleep filling toward the morning. The fill visibly drops at that instant. That is the same live reclassification ADR-0033 already placed at that hour, now with a bar attached to it.
- **A Household that keeps no Night Period reads a bedtime as a long Nap until it crosses the Day Start.** The same quiet default as before: nothing is invented about when its night begins, and the bar flips once the hour and a half is up. Stating a Night Start is what fixes it, and the Settings hint says so.
- **`dueAt` on the sleep column now means the end of the current state** — down at the Wake Window's end while she is awake, up at the allowance's end while she sleeps — and `kind` says which measure the running case used. The Hub's `wake_window_up` keeps its meaning and stays unknown while she sleeps, so the Home Assistant integration needs no change. The running Sleep's instant is not a *due*: the next-due marker on the Feed column ignores it.
- **`PROTOCOL_VERSION` goes to 3**, for the reason ADR-0016 gave: a client that predates this coerces the unknown `nap` activity to `feed`, and then the Feed Interval lookup can pick it and a Parent editing that field can overwrite it. Old Devices keep logging locally and keep their outbox; they stop pushing until the PWA update lands.
