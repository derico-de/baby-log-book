# Pilot Households stay free forever, as Guest Households

When billing arrives, the friends' pilot Households do not convert onto the Trial or any founder deal — they are grandfathered free, permanently. The alternatives were a standard fresh Trial at launch or an operator-extended Trial as a thank-you; both would have kept a single billing lifecycle and turned the friends into the first pricing test. We chose the promise over the signal: the pilot was offered as informal and free, the friends are lending their family's data to shake the product out, and at a planning envelope of hundreds of Households a handful of free ones cost nothing. The state this creates gets a name — **Guest Household**: hosted, Plan-less, by personal arrangement of the operator, never on Trial and never Lapsed — because it outlives the launch moment (the operator's own family plausibly lives in it too) and billing must model it as a third standing rather than a temporary pre-launch condition.

## Consequences

- **Billing has three standings, not two.** A hosted Household is on a Plan (or its Trial), Lapsed, or a Guest. The Guest standing is operator-granted, has no billing surface, no Payer and no clock.
- **The promise is per-Household, not per-person.** Households founded during the pilot stay free; anything founded after launch is a standard, paying affair — the operator may still *choose* to mint a new Guest Household, but the promise doesn't oblige it.
- **Billing launch is a non-event inside a pilot Household.** No banner, no date, no state change — just a personal thank-you message outside the app: it's a paid service for new folks now, you stay free.
- **The invitation sentence is safe to say now.** "Free for you, forever — and your data is always yours to take and self-host" can be said at pilot-invitation time precisely because this decision is made.
- **The pricing signal is knowingly forfeited.** Forever-free friends validate usability, never willingness to pay. Public-launch decisions that lean on pilot feedback must not read it as pricing evidence.
